Setup guide

Welcome to Meesho Ads Spend Monitor

It checks your Meesho ad spend every 15–25 minutes in the background and alerts you when a campaign burns money. It never logs in for you and never stores your password. It only changes campaigns if you turn on Auto-stop, and then only to pause them.

~5 minSetup per Chrome profile
15–25 minBetween checks
AutoSteps tick off as you finish
Step 1

Pin the extension to your toolbar

  1. Click the puzzle-piece icon (Extensions) at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Find Meesho Ads Spend Monitor and click the pin icon.
  3. The teal icon now sits in your toolbar. Click it any time to see status, run a check, or resume.

The icon badge tells you the state at a glance: no badge = healthy, SET = setup incomplete, ! = paused and needs you.

Step 2

Log in to Meesho and open the Ads page

Not detected
  1. In this Chrome profile, log in to supplier.meesho.com as usual, with your phone or email and OTP.
  2. Go to Ads in the left menu so the URL looks like /panel/v3/new/ads/<id>/advertisement.
  3. Keep that tab open. The extension detects your account from it. You never type an ID or password here.
Step 3

Copy the campaign-list request body (one time)

Missing

Meesho's Ads page sends a small JSON request to load your campaigns. The extension needs that same request body so it asks for exactly what the page asks for. You only do this once. You can then copy it to other profiles with Export/Import.

  1. On the Meesho Ads page, press F12 (or Ctrl+Shift+I) to open DevTools.
  2. Open the Network tab, then type fetch-campaign-list in the filter box.
  3. Reload the page (F5). A row named fetch-campaign-list appears. Click it.
  4. Open the Payload tab, click view source, and copy the JSON. It's short, about 80 characters.
  5. Paste it below and click Save body.
Only copy the Payload. Never copy from the Headers tab or share cookies such as connect.sid, s_id or _abck with anyone. They give full access to your account.
The list shows only 10 campaigns. Do I need pagination?

If the payload has a field like page, offset or page_number, enter its name under Settings → Advanced → Pagination field. The extension then fetches up to 3 pages, and only when that's needed to see all LIVE and today's campaigns.

Tip: if the payload has a status filter, setting it to LIVE is the most efficient choice.

Step 4

Set your spend limits

Not set

Set at least one of per-campaign or daily total. All amounts are in ₹. Leave a field empty to turn that rule off. Every campaign is checked separately; only the daily total adds campaigns together.

Campaign-wise limits (optional)

Set a different limit, expected ROI or auto-stop choice for any single campaign. Empty fields use the defaults above.

Step 5

Choose where alerts go

Not set

Telegram alerts on your phone (recommended)

  1. In Telegram, open @BotFather and send /newbot. Pick any name and username.
  2. BotFather replies with a token like 123456:ABC-xyz…. Paste it below.
  3. Open your new bot in Telegram and send it any message, such as /start.
  4. Click Detect chat ID. The extension finds your chat automatically.

Using several shops? Use the same bot and chat ID in every profile. Each message starts with the shop name.

Step 6

Send a test alert

Not sent

You should see a desktop pop-up, a Telegram message, or both. If nothing arrives, see Troubleshooting below.

Step 7

Run your first check

Not run

This reads your campaign list once, through the open Meesho tab, and shows what it found. After that, checks run on their own.

Step 8 · optional

Add your other shops (Chrome profiles)

  1. Here, open Settings → Export settings. This saves your limits and request body to a file. The Telegram token is left out on purpose.
  2. Switch to the next Chrome profile, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and select the same extension folder.
  3. In the new profile, log in to that shop's Meesho account and open its Ads page (step 2).
  4. Open Settings → Import settings, then re-enter the Telegram token and give the shop its own label.
  5. Set a different Start offset per profile (0, 7, 14, 21 minutes…) so the shops don't all check at the same moment.

Using it every day

Keep these running

  • Chrome stays open, with each profile window open (it can be minimized).
  • The Meesho Ads tab stays logged in. If you close it, the extension reopens it as a small pinned tab (Auto-open tab setting).
  • The PC doesn't sleep. On Windows: Settings → System → Power → Sleep → Never while plugged in.
  • Checks only run during your active hours (default 08:00–23:00 IST).

What each alert means

AlertFires whenSuggested action
Per-campaign limitA LIVE campaign has spent your per-campaign ₹ limit or moreOpen Meesho Ads and decide whether to pause it
Budget % usedA LIVE campaign has used your warning % of its budget (default 80%)Check whether it is converting before it hits 100%
Spend with 0 ordersSpend reached your wasted-spend ₹ amount with no ordersUsually worth pausing or changing the catalog
ROI below expectedA LIVE campaign's ROI is under its expected ROI after the minimum spendReview the catalog and bid
Campaign stoppedAuto-stop is on and the campaign hit its limit with low ROI (or no expected ROI set)Nothing; it's already paused. Restart it in Meesho if you disagree
Could not stopAuto-stop tried to pause a campaign but Meesho refused or was unreachablePause it manually right away
Daily limit crossedToday's campaigns together crossed the daily totalReview all of today's campaigns
Monitoring pausedMeesho logged you out, blocked a request, or showed a captchaLog in manually, then click Resume in the popup
HeartbeatOnce a day (default 21:00): proof the monitor is alive, with today's spendNothing. If it doesn't arrive, check that Chrome and the PC are on

Each alert is sent once. You get it again only if spend rises by another ₹50 (Re-alert step setting). Alert memory resets at midnight IST.

The toolbar popup

  • Status: Healthy, Setup needed, or Paused, with the last error if there is one.
  • Live campaigns and today's spend from the most recent check.
  • Check now runs a check immediately. Use it sparingly, because the low frequency keeps your account safe.
  • Resume appears after a pause. Click it only after you have logged back in to Meesho.

Troubleshooting

Badge shows “SET” or status says “Open the Meesho Ads page once…”
Open the Meesho Ads page in this profile (step 2). The account is detected from the page URL. If you use a different Meesho URL, open the Ads section from the Meesho menu.
“Request body not configured” or “not valid JSON”
Repeat step 3. Paste only the Payload JSON, which starts with { and ends with }. To check that alerts work without it, tick Use demo data.
Monitoring paused: session expired or blocked
Meesho logged you out, or its bot protection challenged the request. Log in again in the Meesho tab and solve any captcha. Then click Resume in the popup. After a simple logout, opening the Ads page while logged in also resumes monitoring. The extension never retries on its own here, to protect your account.
Test alert: no desktop pop-up
Windows: Settings → System → Notifications → allow Google Chrome, and turn off Focus assist. macOS: System Settings → Notifications → Google Chrome → Allow. Also check that chrome://settings/content/notifications isn't blocking all notifications.
Telegram: “chat not found” or Detect finds nothing
Send any message to your bot first. Bots can't message you until you do. Then click Detect again. For groups, add the bot to the group and send a message there. If the token was rejected, copy it again from BotFather without extra spaces.
Alerts stopped and no heartbeat arrived
Usually Chrome was closed, the PC slept, or it's outside your active hours. Open the popup to see the last check time and the next scheduled check.
Spend numbers look different from the Meesho dashboard
The monitor uses budget_utilised from the same API the Ads page uses, so small differences are usually just timing between checks. “Today” is based on each campaign's start date in IST.
Your account stays safe: the extension reads your campaign list only through your own open Meesho tab, at most about 3 times an hour. It stops completely if Meesho shows any sign of blocking. It never sees your password or OTP, and it never edits or creates campaigns. It pauses campaigns only when you turn on Auto-stop.
